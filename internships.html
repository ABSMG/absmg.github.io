How to Find a Good Internship

1. Know Your Field

Start by identifying the career field you want to explore. This makes it easier to find internships that match your education and interests.

2. Use Reliable Sources

Look for opportunities through company websites, universities, government institutions and established international organizations.

3. Prepare Your CV

Keep your CV clear and focused. Include your education, relevant skills, projects, volunteering and other useful experience.

4. Prepare a Professional Application

Read the requirements carefully before applying. Make sure your application documents are complete and accurate.

5. Avoid Internship Scams

Be careful with opportunities that demand unusual payments, sensitive information or promises that seem unrealistic. Verify the organization and opportunity before submitting personal information.

Verify Before You Apply

OpportunityBridge provides links and resources for discovering opportunities. Always review the original organization or employer website and confirm current requirements, deadlines and application instructions before applying.
