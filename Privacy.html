Privacy Policy

How OpportunityBridge handles information, cookies, advertising technologies and external services.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to OpportunityBridge. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our website may use information and technologies such as cookies.

This Privacy Policy explains the general ways information may be handled when you visit and use OpportunityBridge.

2. Information We Collect

OpportunityBridge is primarily an informational website. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information simply for browsing our pages.

Depending on how you interact with the website, limited technical information may be processed automatically by web hosting, analytics, advertising or security services.

Such information may include items such as browser type, device information, approximate technical information, pages visited and general usage information.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that may be stored on a visitor's browser. Websites and third-party services may use cookies for functionality, measurement, security and advertising.

OpportunityBridge may use or allow third-party services that use cookies or similar technologies.

Important:

Google states that publishers using AdSense must clearly disclose the use of cookies and third-party advertising technologies in their privacy policy.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

OpportunityBridge may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising partners.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies can allow Google and its partners to provide advertisements that may be more relevant to users based on their browsing activity.

Google may also use technologies such as cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers as described in its applicable policies.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use websites or apps that use its services, please visit:

Google — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services Google Ads Settings

5. Personalized Advertising

Depending on available settings, location and applicable privacy requirements, advertising displayed on the website may be personalized or non-personalized.

Users can manage certain Google advertising preferences through Google's Ads Settings.

Where applicable, users may also be presented with consent choices through advertising or consent-management systems.

6. Third-Party Websites and Links

OpportunityBridge may link to external websites operated by universities, organizations, companies, job boards, learning platforms and other third parties.

Once you leave OpportunityBridge and visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policy of any external website before submitting personal information.

7. Contact Information

If you contact OpportunityBridge, information you provide may be used only for the purpose of responding to your request or providing the requested assistance.

Please avoid sending passwords, banking PINs, identity document numbers or other highly sensitive information unless it is specifically necessary and the communication channel is appropriate and secure.

For general questions, please visit our Contact page.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain a safe website environment. However, no internet transmission or website can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should also take reasonable precautions when submitting information online, especially when following links to external websites.

9. Children's Privacy

OpportunityBridge is an informational resource website intended for a general audience. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through dedicated child-directed features.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices

Depending on your browser, device and location, you may have controls that allow you to manage cookies and other privacy settings.

  • You may manage cookies through your browser settings.
  • You may manage certain Google advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings.
  • Where required, applicable consent choices may be presented through a consent-management system.
  • You may choose not to provide information when it is not required.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, advertising technologies, legal requirements or other operational practices.

When changes are made, the updated date at the top of this page may also be changed.

12. Important Disclaimer

This Privacy Policy is provided to explain our general privacy practices. It is not legal advice and does not replace advice from a qualified privacy professional or lawyer regarding laws applicable to your specific situation.
