About OpportunityBridge

OpportunityBridge is a digital platform created to make useful opportunities easier to discover.

We bring together scholarships, jobs, internships, fellowships, grants, online learning resources and digital opportunities in one simple place.

Our goal is to help students, graduates and job seekers discover opportunities that can support their education, skills and careers.

Opportunities may come from Tanzania, Africa and international organizations, universities, companies and other reputable sources around the world.
