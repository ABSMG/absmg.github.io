Career Skills

Build practical skills that can help you prepare for education and employment.

What Are Career Skills?

Career skills are abilities that help people communicate, work with others, solve problems and prepare for professional opportunities.

Important Career Skills

  • Communication
  • CV and resume writing
  • Interview preparation
  • Teamwork
  • Problem solving
  • Time management
  • Professional communication
  • Networking
  • Research skills
  • Digital literacy

Career Development

Developing career skills is a continuous process. Practice your communication, learn new digital tools, build projects and look for opportunities to gain experience.
