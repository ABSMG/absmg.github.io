How to Find the Right Scholarship

Finding a scholarship is not only about searching for a programme that offers funding. Applicants should also check whether they meet the academic, nationality, age, programme and application requirements.

  • Read the official scholarship announcement carefully.
  • Check the required academic qualifications.
  • Confirm the eligible countries or nationalities.
  • Check the application opening and closing dates.
  • Prepare required documents before starting the application.
  • Always verify important information on the official scholarship or university website.
Important: Scholarship availability, deadlines, eligibility and funding can change. OpportunityBridge provides information to help students discover opportunities, but applicants should always verify the latest requirements and deadline on the official source before applying.

What Does "Fully Funded" Mean?

The term "fully funded" does not always mean exactly the same thing for every scholarship. Some programmes may cover tuition, accommodation, living support, travel or other costs, while others may cover only selected expenses.

For this reason, students should read the official funding description carefully before making an application or financial decision.
