Top Scholarships for Tanzanian Students in 2026/2027

Discover scholarship opportunities, study-abroad funding, and trusted resources for Tanzanian and African students.

Published by OpportunityBridge • 2026/2027 Scholarship Guide

Finding the right scholarship can make higher education much more affordable for students in Tanzania. Every year, universities, governments, foundations, and international organizations provide scholarships for students who want to study in Tanzania, Africa, or abroad.

For Tanzanian students, scholarships can help cover tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses, travel costs, books, and other education-related expenses. Some opportunities provide full funding, while others cover only part of the cost of study.

Important: Scholarship requirements, deadlines, and funding benefits can change from one application cycle to another. Always verify the latest information on the official scholarship provider's website before applying.

What Is a Scholarship?

A scholarship is financial support provided to a student to help pay for education. Unlike a normal loan, many scholarships do not need to be repaid, although the conditions depend on the scholarship provider.

Scholarships may be offered based on academic performance, financial need, leadership, community involvement, specific fields of study, nationality, or other eligibility requirements.

1. HESLB Support for Tanzanian Students

The Higher Education Students' Loans Board (HESLB) is one of the major sources of higher-education financial support for eligible Tanzanian students.

Students planning to study at eligible higher-learning institutions in Tanzania should check the latest HESLB application guidelines, eligibility requirements, deadlines, and loan categories.

Official resource:

Visit the official HESLB website

2. DAAD Scholarships

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) supports international students and researchers through a wide range of scholarship and academic exchange opportunities.

Tanzanian students interested in studying in Germany should explore DAAD's current scholarship database and carefully check the requirements for each individual programme.

Official resource:

Visit DAAD

3. Study in Japan Scholarships

Japan provides opportunities for international students through different scholarship and study programmes. Tanzanian students interested in Japan should research available programmes and application procedures carefully.

The Japanese government scholarship programme commonly known as MEXT is one important opportunity students may encounter when researching study opportunities in Japan.

Official resource:

Visit Study in Japan

4. Mastercard Foundation Opportunities

The Mastercard Foundation works with educational institutions and partners to support young people in Africa and other regions.

Scholarship availability varies by partner institution and programme. Students should therefore check the current opportunities and eligibility requirements rather than assuming that every programme is available to all applicants.

Official resource:

Visit Mastercard Foundation

5. Chevening Scholarships

Chevening is a major international scholarship programme associated with the UK government. It provides opportunities for eligible applicants from participating countries to pursue postgraduate study in the United Kingdom.

Because eligibility and application requirements are specific, prospective applicants should review the official Chevening information before preparing an application.

Official resource:

Visit Chevening

6. University-Specific Scholarships

Many universities provide their own scholarships or financial-aid programmes for international students. These opportunities can be particularly useful for students planning to study outside Tanzania.

When searching for university scholarships, visit the official university website and look for sections such as:

  • International Students
  • Scholarships
  • Financial Aid
  • Funding Opportunities
  • Admissions

Never rely only on social-media posts or forwarded messages when applying for scholarships. Always confirm the opportunity through an official source.

7. Scholarships Across Africa

Tanzanian students can also find scholarship opportunities offered by African universities, foundations, governments, development organizations, and international institutions.

Depending on the programme, opportunities may be available for undergraduate, postgraduate, research, professional development, or short-term study.

How to Find Scholarships Successfully

Scholarship searching becomes easier when you use a consistent strategy. Instead of waiting until the deadline is close, start researching opportunities early.

  1. Identify the country or university where you want to study.
  2. Choose your preferred field of study.
  3. Search for scholarships matching your education level.
  4. Read the eligibility requirements carefully.
  5. Prepare your academic documents.
  6. Prepare a strong personal statement where required.
  7. Check application deadlines.
  8. Apply through the official application platform.
  9. Keep copies of your submitted documents.

Documents You May Need

Scholarship requirements vary, but applicants may commonly be asked for some of the following documents:

  • Academic certificates or transcripts
  • Identification documents
  • Academic references
  • Personal statement
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Proof of language ability where required
  • Admission letter where required
  • Other programme-specific documents

How to Increase Your Chances of Winning a Scholarship

A strong application is more than just good grades. Scholarship committees may consider academic performance, motivation, leadership, community involvement, career goals, and how well the applicant matches the objectives of the programme.

1. Start Early

Starting early gives you time to collect documents, write strong application essays, and correct mistakes before the deadline.

2. Follow Instructions

Read every application requirement carefully. Missing one required document can affect an otherwise strong application.

3. Write a Strong Personal Statement

Explain your academic background, goals, motivation, achievements, and how the scholarship will support your future plans.

4. Apply to Multiple Suitable Opportunities

Do not depend on only one scholarship. Research several legitimate opportunities that match your qualifications and apply before their deadlines.

How to Avoid Scholarship Scams

Unfortunately, fake scholarship advertisements can appear online. Students should be careful before sending money or personal information to an unknown organisation.

  • Check whether the scholarship has an official website.
  • Verify the organisation offering the scholarship.
  • Check the application deadline and requirements.
  • Be cautious of unrealistic promises.
  • Never assume an opportunity is legitimate simply because it is shared online.
  • Use official sources whenever possible.

What Does "Fully Funded Scholarship" Mean?

A fully funded scholarship generally means that the scholarship provides substantial financial support for the main costs associated with the programme.

However, the exact benefits differ between scholarships. One programme may cover tuition, accommodation and living expenses, while another may provide additional support such as travel or health insurance.

Therefore, always read the official funding details before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Tanzanian students get scholarships abroad?

Yes. Tanzanian students can apply for international scholarships when they meet the eligibility requirements of the specific programme.

Can Form Six graduates apply for scholarships?

Some undergraduate scholarship programmes accept students who have completed secondary education, while others require admission to a university first. Always check the specific requirements.

Are all scholarships fully funded?

No. Some scholarships are fully funded, while others provide partial financial support.

Where should I apply for scholarships?

Whenever possible, apply through the official website of the scholarship provider, university, government agency, or recognised organisation.

Final Thoughts

Scholarships can open important educational opportunities for Tanzanian students who want to continue their studies in Tanzania, Africa, or abroad.

The most important strategy is to start early, research carefully, prepare strong documents, follow application instructions, and verify every opportunity through an official source.
