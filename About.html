About Tanzania Digital Hub

Tanzania Digital Hub is an independent online platform created to help people in Tanzania discover useful education, career and digital opportunities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make useful information easier to discover by bringing scholarships, jobs, internships, online courses and digital learning resources together in one place.

What We Provide

We share information and resources relating to scholarships, employment opportunities, internships, online learning, digital skills and other useful opportunities.

Our Goal

We aim to become a trusted digital resource for Tanzanian students, graduates, job seekers and anyone looking to improve their skills and discover new opportunities.

Information Accuracy

We encourage visitors to verify important information such as deadlines, eligibility requirements, fees and application instructions on the official website of the organization providing an opportunity.
