How to Search for Jobs

A good job search starts with understanding the type of position you want and checking reliable sources regularly.

  • Identify your skills, qualifications and career interests.
  • Prepare an updated CV and relevant application documents.
  • Search through official employer or recruitment sources.
  • Read the complete job description before applying.
  • Check application deadlines and required qualifications.
  • Keep records of positions you have applied for.
Job Safety: Be careful with job advertisements that request money, passwords, banking details or other sensitive information before employment is verified. Always confirm the employer and use official application channels whenever possible.
