Computer Skills

Build essential computer skills for school, work and the digital world.

What Are Computer Skills?

Computer skills are the abilities needed to use computers and digital tools effectively. They are useful for students, job seekers, entrepreneurs and professionals.

Important Computer Skills to Learn

  • Computer basics and operating systems
  • Microsoft Word
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Microsoft PowerPoint
  • Internet and web browsing
  • Email communication
  • File and folder management
  • Online collaboration tools
  • Typing and keyboard skills
  • Digital safety and cybersecurity awareness

Why Computer Skills Matter

Strong computer skills can help you study more effectively, apply for jobs, work online, communicate professionally and manage digital information.
